# Lowercase Text Poster Ideas

A short guide to making calm, readable posters, story posts and invitations from lowercase text. If you want to try a layout quickly, a [brat generator](https://bratgeneratortool.com/) can help you preview a plain text style before you design anything bigger.

## Why lowercase works

Lowercase lines feel casual and direct. With no capital letters competing for attention, the eye reads the message in one pass, which suits short slogans and single sentences.

## Colour and contrast

Pick one flat background and one text colour. Keep contrast high so the words stay readable on a phone screen. Soft greens, plain whites and muted greys all work well when the text is dark.

## Layout basics

Keep the text block left aligned or centred, but not both. Leave generous margins and use a single typeface. Short lines of three to six words read best.

## Where to use it

Story posts, party invitations, event reminders, cover images and simple announcement cards all suit this look.
